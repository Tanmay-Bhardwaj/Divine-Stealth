## The Pitch in 30 Seconds

You are a god reincarnated into a modern metropolis, bound by an ancient oath: **no mortal shall ever know your true nature.** Aethelburg is rotting — corrupt landlords, laundering banks, blackout cartels, cults selling fake miracles. You have the freedom of a GTA sandbox (drive, climb, infiltrate, socialize) and four *subtle* powers that must always look like luck. Redistribute wealth, save lives, topple the corrupt — and if a single viral video proves what you are, **the Oath breaks and the story resets**, though the good you did remains in the world.
