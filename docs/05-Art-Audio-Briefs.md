# DIVINE STEALTH: REBORN — Art & Audio Direction Briefs (v1.0)

For concept artists, environment teams, character artists, composers, and sound designers.

---

## 1. Visual Pillars

1. **Grounded Realism, Cinematic Eye** — a believable modern metropolis shot like a prestige thriller: long lenses, shallow depth in conversations, wide anamorphic for skyline beats.
2. **The Almost-Invisible Divine** — miracles must sit at the edge of perception. If a player screenshots a Nudge, it should look like nothing. VFX bar: *deniable in a still frame.*
3. **The City Heals** — Goodness is rendered, not narrated: light temperature, foliage, paint, posture of crowds.

### Verbal mood board — Aethelburg
- *Reference blend:* the vertical finance-glass of a fictionalized Frankfurt/Chicago core; brick-and-fog river old-town à la Porto/Edinburgh closes; sodium-lit dock sprawl from 70s thriller cinematography; a media district of LED billboards reflecting in wet asphalt.
- *Palette by district:* Oldbridge — umber brick, brass, fog blue. Shallows — pre-Goodness: dead teal, sodium orange, blackout black; post-Renewal: string-light gold, mural color. Coinwharf — steel, glass green, cold white. Hearthvale — beige vinyl siding hiding white-and-gold cult kitsch. Crown Terrace — granite, oxidized bronze, flag red.
- *Weather is a character:* rain as the god's ally (fewer eyes, blurred lenses) — make rain *beautiful and safe-feeling*, inverting genre convention.

### Goodness state-swap spec (per district, 4 visual states: 0/25/50/75-100)
Prop-set swaps (trash→planters, plywood→shopfronts), lighting warm-shift (+300 K, +10% bounce at Renewed), crowd animation sets (hunched → open posture), audio bed swap (see §4). Budget: 4 state variants of ~15% of props per district; the rest via lighting/decals/crowd.

## 2. Character Design Brief

### Protagonist "Ash"
- **Design law: forgettable on purpose.** Median height/build; symmetrical but unremarkable face; the artist's test is "could you describe them to a sketch artist after 10 seconds? You shouldn't be able to."
- Wardrobe = civilian capsule sets per cover (temp-worker business casual, auditor grey suit, Meridian workwear, Seeker linen). **No hero silhouette, no signature color.** The only recurring motif: a worn cord bracelet (the Oath knot) — visible only in cutscene close-ups.
- Eyes: in 1% of idle blinks and one authored cutscene (Mission 5 mirror pit), a too-old stillness. Never glow. Never.

### Key NPCs (one-line art directions)
- **Imelda** — warmth in a storm; cardigan armor; hands that have buried people.
- **Priya Nair** — deadline posture, ink-stained optimism, press lanyard worn like a medal.
- **Mara Voss** — the anti-Ash: *extremely* memorable; red coat, asymmetric bob; her murder-board aesthetic bleeds into her framing (always shot through glass, strings, reflections).
- **Brother Solon** — thrift-store messiah: immaculate white suit, scuffed shoes (the tell in his character reveal close-up).
- **Elena Marsh** — precision under fear; everything she wears has pockets; counts things when anxious.

## 3. VFX Brief — The Deniable Miracle
| Power | Visual | Hard don'ts |
|---|---|---|
| Nudge | ≤ 6 frames: a dust-mote drift reversal, a half-second exposure lift in a 2 m radius. Player-camera-only; witnesses' POV renders nothing | No particle trails, no glyphs, no slow-mo |
| Insight | Desaturation + one breathing highlight; glyphs are UI, not world | No eye glow, no scan lines |
| Mend | Nothing at the wound. Warmth conveyed by *audio* + the healed NPC's animation easing | No light from palms, ever |
| Veil | World-invisible; only camera-feed monitors show a smear | — |
| Oath Break cinematic | The ONE budgeted spectacle: choir + every screen in view + a 2-second impossible stillness of the crowd | Reserve all "divine" grammar for this |

## 4. Audio Direction

### Music — adaptive score spec
- **Ensemble identity:** chamber strings + prepared piano + analog synth pads (the mortal city) / a cappella choir in a lost-language conlang (the divine — used ONLY at oath/restart/shrine beats; ≤ 6 min total in Act I so it never cheapens).
- **Vertical layering states:** Ambient (2 stems) → Scout (+pulse stem) → Execute (+ostinato) → Pursuit (full kit, tempo-synced to police tier) → Aftermath (strings thin out; if Clean Escape, resolve to major-key sparse piano = the "no one will ever know" motif).
- **Goodness themes:** each district owns a 4-bar folk-flavored motif; at Renewal it enters the ambient bed played by street musicians (diegetic conversion — the city literally sings the score).
- **The Voss cue:** a dry, un-reverbed col legno tick whenever she's near — the only "dry" sound in a wet mix; players learn to fear dryness.
- Reference blend for composer: *the intimacy of a Jóhannsson-style string room, heist-jazz restraint, minimal choir used like a scalpel.*

### SFX & ambience
- **City as instrument:** 4-layer district beds (traffic/human/infrastructure/weather) that re-mix with Goodness (blackout Shallows = generators + distant arguments; Renewed = kids, markets, a basketball bounce — callback to Mission 3's cutscene).
- **Tension telemetry via diegesis:** rising Suspicion is *heard* before seen — phone notification pings from bystanders increase in density; radio chatter mentions "that lucky stranger"; at global 85+, conversations hush briefly when the player walks past (crowd VO ducking, −6 dB, 1.5 s).
- Miracle SFX: Nudge = a room-tone "settle" (like a house sigh); Mend = sub-audible 40 Hz warmth + fabric; Veil = nothing in-world, monitor static only.
- **Restart silence:** the Oath Break's first 5 seconds are the only true digital silence in the game. Protect it in the mix spec.

### Voice direction (if full VO)
- Ash: low-energy warmth, never quippy; the performance secret given to the actor: *"you have apologized to people for 4,000 years."*
- Crowd systems: 3 tiers of recorded bystander reaction VO keyed to Anomaly Score (murmur / point-and-film / panic) — this is a gameplay-legibility channel, cast and direct it as such.
